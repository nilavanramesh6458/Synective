Understanding “Deferrals” in Business Central: Spread Out Revenues & Expenses Efficiently

In Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central, deferrals help businesses accurately recognize revenues and expenses over multiple accounting periods. Instead of recording an entire transaction at once, deferrals allow costs and income to be distributed across the periods where they actually apply.

This ensures financial statements provide a more accurate representation of a company’s performance and prevents large fluctuations caused by payments being recorded too early or too late.

What Are Deferrals?

Deferrals are used when a business receives or pays for something that applies to multiple accounting periods. Instead of posting the full amount immediately, Business Central can automatically spread the amount across the appropriate periods.

For example, if a company pays for a one-year insurance policy upfront, the expense should not be recognized entirely in the month it was paid. Instead, the cost should be divided and recognized each month throughout the year.

How Deferrals Work in Business Central

Business Central uses deferral templates to define how transactions should be distributed over time. Users can specify the deferral period, starting date, ending date, and calculation method.

Once configured, Business Central automatically creates the necessary journal entries for each accounting period, reducing manual calculations and improving accuracy.

Benefits of Using Deferrals

Using deferrals provides several advantages, including improved financial accuracy, easier month-end closing, and reduced administrative effort.

Businesses can better match expenses and revenues with the periods in which they occur, resulting in clearer financial reporting and better decision-making.

Common Examples of Deferrals

Common examples include prepaid insurance, subscription services, rent payments, maintenance agreements, and annual software licenses.

Instead of recognizing these payments immediately, businesses can use deferrals to distribute the costs evenly across the appropriate accounting periods.

Deferrals are an important feature in Business Central that helps organizations maintain accurate financial records. By automatically spreading revenues and expenses over time, businesses can improve reporting accuracy and simplify accounting processes.
