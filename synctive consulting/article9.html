Streamlining Purchase Approvals using Workflows

Managing purchase approvals manually can slow down operations and increase the risk of errors. Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central provides built-in workflow capabilities that help automate and streamline approval processes, ensuring efficiency and control.

What are Purchase Approval Workflows?

Purchase approval workflows in Business Central allow organizations to define rules that automatically route purchase documents (such as purchase orders or invoices) for approval. These workflows ensure that the right people review and approve transactions before they are finalized.

Key Benefits

  • Automates approval processes and reduces manual work
  • Ensures compliance with company policies
  • Improves visibility into approval status
  • Reduces delays in purchasing operations

How It Works

Workflows are configured by defining conditions that trigger approval requests. For example, a purchase order exceeding a certain amount can automatically require approval from a manager. Notifications are sent to approvers, and the system tracks each step until the process is complete.

Steps to Set Up Purchase Approval Workflows

  1. Open Workflows from the Search function
  2. Click on "New Workflow from Template" to create a new workflow from the Microsoft provided template, for ‘Purchase Invoice Approval Workflow’
  3. Set the conditions that trigger the workflow. For example, you might specify that the workflow activates when a purchase order exceeds a certain amount.
  4. Enable the workflow
  5. To set the Approvers, start with ‘Add Record Restriction’
  6. The below options will ensure that the approval requests go in a chain to the approvers until the amount threshold is reached, as set up in the ‘Approval User Setup’
  7. Enable the Workflow to use this setup for all new documents moving forward.

Implementing purchase approval workflows in Business Central helps organizations maintain control over spending while improving efficiency. By automating approvals, businesses can reduce bottlenecks, increase accountability, and ensure smoother operations.
