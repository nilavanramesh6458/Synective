Bulk Update Item Prices in Business Central

Need to update item prices in bulk, such as applying a percentage increase across your inventory? Microsoft Business Central provides powerful tools to simplify this process and ensure your pricing stays accurate and competitive.

Using the Sales Price Worksheet, you can quickly adjust prices across multiple items without manually updating each one.

Open the Sales Price Worksheet

Start by searching for and opening the Sales Price Worksheet page in Business Central. This is where all bulk pricing adjustments are managed.

Suggest Sales Price Changes

Use the Suggest Sales Price on Wksh. action to adjust current sales prices by a percentage. This can also be used to copy pricing agreements to new customers, groups, or promotions.

You can define how prices should change and apply consistent updates across your product range.

Adjust Item Prices

Use the Suggest Item Price on Wksh. option to change item prices by a percentage or create pricing for different currencies, units of measure, or conditions.

Fill in the required fields such as Sales Type, Sales Code, and set your desired adjustment values.

Generate and Apply New Prices

Enter the necessary item details and enable Create New Prices if you are generating new pricing entries.

Click OK to populate the worksheet with suggested changes, then use the Implement Price Changes action to apply updates.

Conclusion

Using the Sales Price Worksheet in Business Central allows businesses to efficiently update item pricing in bulk. This ensures prices remain accurate, competitive, and consistent across your inventory while saving time and reducing manual effort.
