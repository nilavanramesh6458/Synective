Validate Purchase Totals Before Posting in Business Central

Tired of mismatches between vendor invoices and your system entries? Business Central now offers a global feature to help ensure accuracy before posting purchase documents.

By enabling "Check Doc. Total Amounts" in the Purchases & Payables Setup, users must enter and validate the total invoice amount (including tax) and Tax amount against the purchase lines.

Why This Feature Matters

Ensure accuracy in purchase invoices by validating that header totals (including VAT) match the calculated line totals.

Step-by-Step Setup

Below is the step-by-step approach on how to test this feature:

Step 1: Open Purchase & Payables Setup

Use the search bar and open Purchase & Payables Setup.

Step 2: Enable Check Doc. Total Amounts

In the General section, toggle on Check Doc. Total Amounts.

Creating a Purchase Invoice

Go to the Purchase Invoices page and click New. Enter:

  • Vendor
  • Dates
  • Line items
  • Quantity
  • Unit Cost
  • VAT Codes

Entering Document Totals

In the General section of the Purchase Invoice, enter:

  • Doc. Amount Incl. Tax – Total invoice amount
  • Doc. Amount Tax – VAT portion

Once the total is entered, the Tax amount auto-fills.

Posting Validation

Click Post. Business Central compares:

  • Entered header totals
  • Calculated totals from lines

If there is a mismatch, an error appears preventing posting.

Conclusion

This small but powerful control reduces human errors, overpayments, and ensures clean financial records.

Whether using Purchase Invoices or Credit Memos, this ensures totals align before posting.
