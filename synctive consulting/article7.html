Set Up and Sync Master Data Across Companies

Managing multiple companies in Microsoft Business Central can become challenging when important information needs to remain consistent across different environments. Master data synchronization helps businesses maintain accurate and up-to-date information across multiple companies.

By synchronizing master data, organizations can reduce duplicate work, improve accuracy, and ensure that all companies are working with the same reliable information.

What is Master Data?

Master data refers to the core information that businesses rely on every day. This includes customers, vendors, items, prices, inventory details, and other important records.

Keeping this information consistent across multiple companies helps prevent errors and improves overall business operations.

Why Synchronize Data Across Companies?

When companies operate separately, maintaining the same information manually can take significant time and create opportunities for mistakes.

Synchronization allows updates made in one company to automatically flow to other connected companies, reducing manual entry and improving efficiency.

Benefits of Master Data Synchronization

Improved Accuracy: Ensures that all companies use consistent and reliable information.

Reduced Manual Work: Eliminates the need to repeatedly enter the same information into multiple companies.

Better Collaboration: Allows teams across different companies to work with the same data.

Common Data That Can Be Synchronized

Businesses can synchronize many types of records, including:

  • Customer records
  • Vendor information
  • Item details
  • Pricing information
  • Inventory data
  • Financial setup information

Improving Business Central Efficiency

Using automated synchronization tools in Business Central allows organizations to spend less time maintaining data and more time focusing on business growth.

A properly configured synchronization process improves reliability, reduces errors, and creates a smoother experience for users across all companies.

Conclusion

Master data synchronization is an essential solution for businesses managing multiple companies in Microsoft Business Central. By keeping information consistent and accurate, organizations can improve productivity and make better decisions.
