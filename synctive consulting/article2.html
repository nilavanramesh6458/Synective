Business Central–Outlook Plug-in

The Business Central Outlook plug-in allows users to access key ERP data directly within Outlook. This integration improves productivity by eliminating the need to switch between systems and enables faster document creation.

Key Benefits

With the Outlook plug-in, users can view customer and vendor details, create sales documents, and manage interactions without leaving their inbox. This helps streamline communication and reduces manual work.

Improved Efficiency

By integrating Business Central with Outlook, teams can respond to emails and generate quotes or invoices in real time. This significantly improves turnaround time and enhances customer service.

Use Cases

Common use cases include creating sales quotes from emails, tracking customer communication, and quickly accessing account information during conversations.

Know how to deploy this add-in

As an admin, you can setup this add-in for all users in a company, or as a user, you can set this up just for you. To know how to deploy this add-in, follow the steps given in Microsoft Learn site linked below.
